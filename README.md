### Research

Medical imaging AI, transfer learning, domain adaptation and uncertainty estimation, mostly on musculoskeletal X-rays. Papers will be listed here as they come out.

Supervised by Dr. Parshuram M. Kamble. Funded by the Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme for Electronics & IT (MeitY, Government of India).

### Selected work

- **[CUK IQAC](https://iqac.cuk.ac.in)**: portal for the university's Internal Quality Assurance Cell, with role-based access and an approval workflow for notices and events. Vue, Express, MySQL. In production.
- **[CELT, CUK](https://celt.cuk.ac.in)**: website for the Centre for English Language Teaching. React, Tailwind. In production.
- **[SecureIn](https://secure-in.vercel.app)**: QR-based gate entry for campus security, with visitor passes, event entries and separate roles for guards and admins. React, Express, MySQL, JWT.
- **[Thindham](https://thindham-food-delivery.vercel.app)**: food-delivery app covering browsing, cart and checkout. MongoDB, Express, React, Node.
- **[ISWAR NGO](https://iswar-ngo.vercel.app)**: website for a non-profit working with underprivileged communities. React, Framer Motion.

Smaller pieces: an [artist landing page](https://artist-landingpage.vercel.app) and a [project dashboard](https://project-management-dashboard-nine.vercel.app).
